SCREEN 2 · WORKED EXAMPLE

Prefixes, stems, and suffixes

Look at umdwebi and abadwebi. Both mean painter, but one is singular and one is plural. The part they share, dwebi, is the stem. What comes before it changes: um- for one painter, aba- for several.

Now look at zingela, to hunt, next to abazingeli, hunters. The stem zingel is the same; the noun ends in -i, the verb in -a.

So a Zulu word for a person who does something is built from three pieces: a prefix that says one or many, a stem, and the ending -i. Pieces that go before the stem are called prefixes, pieces after it suffixes.

Try it: killer is umbulali, so killers is aba + bulali = ababulali, and to kill is bulal + a = bulala.
