AI & LANGUAGE LEARNING

Can AI help us learn how languages work?

We study whether an AI tutor helps complete beginners learn to analyse the structure of words in languages they have never seen. Learners solve short puzzles in several unfamiliar languages, in the style of Linguistics Olympiad problems. They learn a reasoning skill that transfers to any language: splitting words into pieces, working out what each piece means, and predicting new forms.

ABOUT THE EXPERIMENT

What are we studying?

We study morphology: how do languages build words out of smaller meaningful pieces (morphemes) such as roots, prefixes, suffixes and infixes?

You will solve short linguistics olympiads-style puzzles involving languages you have never seen before. The goal is not for you to learn a particular language, but to develop reasoning skills that can be applied to unfamiliar languages.

THE AI TUTOR

How does the technology work?

The experiment uses an AI language model as an interactive tutor. The tutor is designed to help learners reason through the problems rather than simply giving them the answers.

It can ask questions, respond to hypotheses and provide progressively stronger hints when a learner gets stuck.

No prior knowledge required

Olympiad problems are designed to be solvable without prior knowledge of any foreign language or advanced linguistic concept, which makes them a clean test of what learners gained from our lesson.
